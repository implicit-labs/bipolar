# Solo Founder Economics

Based on Pieter Levels' approach to building a $3M+/year business with zero employees, zero VC funding, and near-100% profit margins. The philosophy: every employee makes your company slower.

---

## Core Stance

> "Every employee makes your company slower."

The default path — raise money, hire a team, scale — is optimized for venture capital returns, not founder happiness or profitability. A solo founder with automation can generate millions while keeping 90%+ margins and total freedom.

---

## Key Principles

### Revenue Over Growth

Charge from day one. Favor recurring subscriptions. $300K/month in revenue with zero employees is more valuable than $3M/month with 50 employees and VC obligations. Profit margins matter more than top-line revenue.

### Automate Instead of Hiring

Cron jobs replace operations staff. AI handles content moderation. Stripe handles billing and invoicing. Scripts handle deployments. Every task that doesn't require creative judgment should be automated.

### The 10,000 Customer Math

You only need 10,000 customers at $100/year for $1M in revenue. At $300/year (Photo AI's price point), you need 3,300 customers. These are small, achievable numbers for niche products.

### Multiple Small Bets Over One Big Bet

Run a portfolio of products. Nomad List + Remote OK + Photo AI + Interior AI = diversified income. If one product dies, others sustain you. This is impossible with VC-backed single-product companies.

### Near-100% Profit Margins

Interior AI has >99% profit margins. No employees, minimal infrastructure costs ($20-100/month VPS), and automated operations mean almost all revenue is profit. This is the mathematical advantage of staying solo.

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Better Alternative |
|---|---|
| Raise VC to scale | Bootstrap and keep 100% equity |
| Hire employees for growth | Automate with scripts and AI |
| Build one big product | Build a portfolio of small products |
| Free tier to grow users | Charge from day one |
| Optimize for growth rate | Optimize for profit margins |
| Enterprise sales team | Self-serve with Stripe |
