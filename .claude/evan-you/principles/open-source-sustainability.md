# Open Source Sustainability

Based on Evan You's 10+ years as an independent OSS maintainer, GitHub README interviews, and reflections on burnout and community building. The philosophy: sustainable open source requires clarity, boundaries, and community.

---

## Core Stance

> "At its core, open source is either your main path, or it's supplementary. It can't be both."

Open source maintenance is primarily community building, not just code. Without clear boundaries and a sustainable funding model, burnout is inevitable. The most important skill is knowing when to step away.

---

## Key Principles

### Clarity of Purpose
Decide early whether open source is your career or your hobby. The in-between space — where it's "sort of" your job but also "sort of" a side project — is where burnout lives. Evan made it his main path in 2016 and structured everything around that decision.

### Work-Life Boundaries
- Fixed 9-6 workday, even as self-employed
- No weekend emails
- Decline speaking engagements to prioritize family
- Extended breaks (several weeks) to prevent burnout
- In-person conferences provide emotional sustenance against online negativity

### Community > Code
Vue's success came from organic community growth — blog posts, Twitter, direct engagement on forums (including Chinese platforms like Zhihu). Never paid advertising. The community IS the product.

### Documentation as Product
Writing comprehensive documentation transforms a personal project into a community-usable tool. Documentation is the primary user interface of an open-source library.

### Sustainable Funding
Started with ~$4,000/month from Patreon and sponsorships. Grew to support a full team. VoidZero (founded 2024, $4.6M seed) represents the evolution: open-source core with commercial extensions.

### Team Building
Assemble talented contributors early. Open source at scale requires delegation. Credit collaborators by name. The project outlives any individual's capacity.

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Better Alternative |
|---|---|
| Working every weekend | Fixed schedule with clear boundaries |
| Saying yes to every conference | Selective attendance, prioritize family |
| Solo maintenance heroics | Build a core team, delegate ownership |
| Free forever with no business model | Open core + commercial extensions (VoidZero/Vite+) |
| Ignoring mental health | Extended breaks, in-person community events |
| Measuring success by stars | Measure by impact on people's careers and products |
